# Vacuum pressure 真空壓力

## Confirm and contain / 確認與隔離
SYNTHETIC DEMO PROCEDURE. For vacuum pressure above 50 mTorr for three samples, keep the simulated lot on hold. Compare pressure readings and alarm timestamps. Record machine ID and maintenance history. Notify the equipment on-call engineer. Do not automatically restart equipment or change process recipes.

## Maintenance review / 保養檢查
An authorized equipment engineer reviews pump status and seal maintenance records. Post-maintenance pressure alarms may suggest a seal issue, but pressure alone does not prove a leak. Physical inspection requires the approved local safety procedure. Recovery needs engineer sign-off and a recorded verification result. The assistant only drafts a simulated ticket.
