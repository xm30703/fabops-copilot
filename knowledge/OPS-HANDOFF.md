# Shift handover 值班交接

## Required fields / 交接欄位
SYNTHETIC DEMO PROCEDURE. Handover includes incident IDs, severity, impact, machine, observed facts, unconfirmed hypotheses, cited SOP, pending draft tickets, approvals and next owners. Distinguish observed facts from guesses. Escalate unresolved P1 incidents to the duty lead. Do not report an incident as resolved without recorded evidence.
